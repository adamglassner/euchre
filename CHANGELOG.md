# Changelog

All notable changes to Euchre are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- **Play Together.** Two to four people can share one computer: a "pass the computer" screen comes up before each person's turn, everyone's cards turn face down in between, and the table turns to face whoever is playing. Pick who partners with whom; computer players fill any empty seats.
- **Online play.** Host a game or join a friend's over your home network or the internet. Everyone plays on their own computer and sees the table from their own seat. The host can switch friends between teams in the lobby, and if someone leaves, a computer player takes over their cards.
- Seven new folks pull up a chair. Every single-player game seats three of the ten at random, each with a name that fits their face: maybe Cousin Kayla, Neighbor Lars and Granny Opal this time, and someone else next time. The title card shows who's coming to the table.
- Pick your own name and face for online and shared-computer games.

### Changed
- Euchre is now a native Windows game written in Go. It no longer opens in a Microsoft Edge window, starts faster and runs in its own resizable window (F11 for full screen).
- Same farm-country cards, kitchen table, music and sounds as before. Your record and settings carry over.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- First release of Euchre: A Midwest Kitchen-Table Game for Windows.
- Full Euchre rules: 24-card deck, right and left bowers, two rounds of bidding, ordering up and picking up, going alone, and play to 5 or 10 points.
- Optional "stick the dealer" rule.
- Partner up with Aunt Barb against Uncle Dale and Grandma June, each with their own Midwest sayings.
- HD farm-country deck drawn in-house: farmer kings with pitchforks, farm-wife queens with cherry pies, hired-hand jacks with sweet corn, and a barn-quilt card back.
- Red gingham kitchen table with a woven placemat, a steaming mug of coffee and a bowl of popcorn.
- Three original tunes: "Friday Night Polka" (accordion and tuba), "County Fair Two-Step" (banjo and guitar) and "Lake Effect Waltz", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for shuffling, dealing, playing cards, winning tricks, calling trump, going alone, marches and euchres.
- Popcorn bursts when your team scores and confetti when you win the game.
- Saved record: games played, won and lost, euchres, marches and best streak.
- Credits & Changelog screen and How to Play guide.
