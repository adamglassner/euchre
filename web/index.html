How to Play Euchre

Four players in two teams: you and Barb (across the table) against Dale and June. The deck is just the 9s through Aces: 24 cards.

  1. The deal. Everyone gets 5 cards. The other 4 make the kitty, and its top card is turned face up.
  2. Making trump. Starting left of the dealer, each player can order up the face-up card (its suit becomes trump and the dealer picks it up, then discards one) or pass. If everyone passes, the card is turned down and each player may name any other suit, or pass. With stick the dealer, the dealer has to name one.
  3. The bowers. The Jack of trump is the right bower (the highest card). The other Jack of the same colour is the left bower (second highest) and counts as trump. Then A, K, Q, 10, 9 of trump.
  4. Playing. The player left of the dealer leads. You must follow suit if you can; otherwise play anything, including trump. The highest trump wins the trick, or else the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.
  5. Scoring. The team that made trump scores 1 point for 3 or 4 tricks and 2 for all 5 (a march). If they take fewer than 3, they're euchred and the other team scores 2.
  6. Going alone. Whoever makes trump can go alone; their partner sits out. A lone march is worth 4 points.
