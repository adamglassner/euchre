# Changelog

All notable changes to Euchre are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- First release of Euchre: A Midwest Kitchen-Table Game for Windows.
- Full Euchre rules: 24-card deck, right and left bowers, two rounds of bidding, ordering up and picking up, going alone, and play to 5 or 10 points.
- Optional "stick the dealer" rule.
- Partner up with Aunt Barb against Uncle Dale and Grandma June, each with their own Midwest sayings.
- HD farm-country deck drawn in-house: farmer kings with pitchforks, farm-wife queens with cherry pies, hired-hand jacks with sweet corn, and a barn-quilt card back.
- Red gingham kitchen table with a woven placemat, a steaming mug of coffee and a bowl of popcorn.
- Three original tunes: "Friday Night Polka" (accordion and tuba), "County Fair Two-Step" (banjo and guitar) and "Lake Effect Waltz", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for shuffling, dealing, playing cards, winning tricks, calling trump, going alone, marches and euchres.
- Popcorn bursts when your team scores and confetti when you win the game.
- Saved record: games played, won and lost, euchres, marches and best streak.
- Credits & Changelog screen and How to Play guide.
